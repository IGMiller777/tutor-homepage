Вопросы для разговорной практики

Главная проблема разговорной практики — не отсутствие слов, а отсутствие второй фразы. Ниже вопросы по уровням и схема, которая помогает развернуть ответ.

Схема ответа: P–R–E–S

  • Point — прямой ответ на вопрос (одно предложение).
  • Reason — почему вы так считаете.
  • Example — конкретный пример из своей жизни.
  • So — вывод или переход обратно к собеседнику.
«Do you enjoy working from home?» → Yes, definitely (P). It saves me almost two hours of commuting every day (R). Last month I used that time to finish an online course (E). So I'd find it hard to go back to the office full-time (S).

A2 — базовый уровень

  • What does your typical morning look like?
  • Who do you spend most of your free time with?
  • What kind of food do you cook at home?
  • How do you usually get to work or to your classes?
  • What did you do last weekend?
  • Which season do you like most, and why?
  • What app do you open first every morning?
  • Do you prefer texting or calling? Why?

B1 — уверенный средний

  • What is one habit you would like to change?
  • How has your city changed in the last five years?
  • Is it better to work in a team or on your own?
  • What is the best piece of advice you have ever received?
  • How do you deal with stress before an important event?
  • Would you rather have more money or more free time?
  • What skill would you like to learn in the next year?
  • How do you decide whether a film is worth watching?

B2 — выше среднего

  • Should companies be allowed to monitor employees' work computers?
  • Do you think remote work makes teams weaker?
  • How much responsibility do individuals have for the climate crisis?
  • Is it fair that some jobs pay ten times more than others?
  • Has social media improved the quality of public discussion?
  • Should university education be free for everyone?
  • What role should the state play in supporting the arts?

C1 — продвинутый

  • To what extent does the language you speak shape the way you think?
  • Is expertise losing its value in an age of instant information?
  • Where would you draw the line between privacy and security?
  • Can a society be both highly competitive and genuinely fair?
  • What is lost when a local language disappears?

Полезные связки для развёрнутого ответа

  • The way I see it, …
  • It depends on whether …
  • To give you an example, …
  • That said, there is another side to it.
  • I'd say it comes down to …
  • Let me put it another way.
